Italiano

Work for 25 minutes. Then the cat takes over.

Maramao is a Pomodoro timer for the Mac menu bar. When the break starts, a cat sits on your window, naps on your keyboard or buries your screen, and doesn't move until the break is over.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Free and open source · macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · Version 0.2.0

Choose your cat

Six ways to be interrupted. Pick one from the menu bar, or let Surprise me choose a different one every break.

  • Cat on your window

    A cat walks across the screen and sits on the window you're working in. Switch windows and it follows you.

  • Meme wall

    Cat memes fill the screen, one after another, until the break is over.

  • Don't wake the cat

    A cat sleeps on your screen. Touch the mouse and it wakes up and gives you a look.

  • Cat invasion

    Cats fall from the top of the screen and pile up until there's nothing left to see.

  • Laser chase

    Your pointer turns into a red laser dot and the cat chases it around the screen.

  • Cat on the keyboard

    A cat falls asleep on the keyboard. Whatever you type comes out as “mmmrrrooow”, and ⌘Q won't save you.

The videos are real cats doing what cats do. In the app the cats are drawn, and you can try every mode right away from Try a cat in the menu, without waiting 25 minutes.

Everything else

A small native app that stays out of your way until it's time to stop.

Menu bar only
A countdown next to the clock. No Dock icon, no extra windows.
Waits for your calls
If the mic or camera is on, or a presentation is fullscreen, the break waits until you're done.
Notices when you're away
Lunch or a locked Mac counts as rest, so you come back to a fresh session.
Snooze
Two more minutes? Send the cat away for 1, 3 or 5 minutes, a few times a day.
Strict mode
Normally you can skip a break after a minute. In strict mode you can't.
Your own timings
Work, short break, long break, and how many sessions before the long one.
Every display
Cats on all your screens, only the main one, or the one with the mouse.
Your cat
Every break you take earns your cat a treat. Skip too many and it sulks.
Shortcuts and automation
Global shortcuts, Shortcuts actions and maramao:// links for Raycast, Alfred or a Stream Deck.
Cat packs
Add your own cats, names, captions and meme pictures with a folder and a JSON file.
Emergency exit
⌃⌥⌘⌫ ends any break at once, strict mode included.
Private
No special permissions, no account, no tracking. In English and Italian.

Install

It takes about a minute.

Download the app

Open the DMG and drag Maramao into Applications. It appears in the menu bar as a small cat.

Download for Mac

If macOS won't open it

Maramao isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch needs one extra step.

  1. Open Maramao once and close the warning with Done.
  2. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the Maramao message.
  3. Confirm with your password.

On macOS 13 and 14, right-click the app and choose Open instead. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Maramao.app.

Terminal

Puts the latest version in Applications and takes care of the first-launch step.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kkratterf/maramao/main/install.sh | sh

You can read the script first.

Homebrew

brew tap kkratterf/maramao https://github.com/kkratterf/maramao && brew install --cask maramao

Questions

Why cats?

Anyone who lives with a cat knows that when it decides you're done, you're done. A notification is easy to ignore. A cat sitting on your document is not.

Does it need any permissions?

No. Maramao doesn't ask for Accessibility or Screen Recording, doesn't read what you type and doesn't send anything anywhere.

Can I skip a break?

Yes. A Skip button shows up after a minute, and you can change that in Settings. Strict mode removes it. Snooze pushes the break back a few minutes instead.

What if a break starts during a call?

It doesn't. While the microphone or camera is in use, or Keynote, PowerPoint, a video or a call app is fullscreen, the break waits a minute at a time, up to 10 minutes by default.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Maramao from its menu and move it to the Bin. Or use one line in Terminal, which removes the settings too:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kkratterf/maramao/main/install.sh | sh -s -- --uninstall

With Homebrew: brew uninstall --zap --cask maramao.

Is it really free?

Yes. Maramao is open source under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub, and pull requests with new cats are welcome.
